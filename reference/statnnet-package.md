# statnnet: statistical selection and interpretation for nnet models

`statnnet` selects parsimonious, single-hidden-layer Gaussian regression
models fitted with
[`nnet::nnet()`](https://rdrr.io/pkg/nnet/man/nnet.html) and provides
regression-style statistical summaries for supported fitted `nnet`
models. Inferential summaries after selection are conditional on the
selected model and are not post-selection inference.
